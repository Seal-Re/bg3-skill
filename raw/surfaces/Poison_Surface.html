Simple Toxin (surface)

From bg3.wiki
(Redirected from Poison Surface)
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Simple Toxin is a surface that deals light poison damage.

Description

Applies Poisoned.

Properties

Type: Surface
 Dippable

Condition: Simple Toxin

 Simple Toxin

Duration: 1 turn

 CON Save (DC 11)

  • Affected entity takes 1d4⁠⁠Poison damage at the end of its next turn.

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

  • Basic Poison

Spells and actions:

  • Chromatic Orb: Poison
  • Sphere of Elemental Balance: Poison
  • Venomous Discharge (Addled Frog)
  • Venomous Discharge (Phase Spider)
  • Venomous Discharge (Phase Spider Matriarch)

Notes

  • Phase Spiders leave behind simple toxin surfaces when struck, instead of blood. It is also spread through their Venomous Discharge attack.
