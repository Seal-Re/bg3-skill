Water (surface)

From bg3.wiki
(Redirected from Water Surface)
Jump to navigation Jump to search
See also: Water

Water is a type of surface that can be created by numerous ways and manipulated by other elements.

Description

Douses unprotected flames. Can be electrified or frozen.

Properties

Type: Surface
 Dippable

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

  • Bubbling Cauldron
  • Carafe of Water
  • Decanter of Nearly Endless Water
  • Holy Water
  • Jug of Water
  • Trident of the Waves
  • Water
  • Water Barrel

Spells and actions:

  • Create Water
  • Slam (Water Elemental)
  • Minor Conjuration: Create Water

Interactions

  • Turns into  Electrified Water for Duration: 2 turns after being exposed to lightning damage. Reverts back to water afterwards.
  • Turns into  Ice for Duration: 2 turns after being exposed to ice damage. Reverts back to water afterwards.
  • Turns into a  Steam Cloud for Duration: 2 turns after being exposed to a fire surface.

Notes

  • As of Patch #6, can be created by interacting with a water pump.
  • Dipping a weapon into this surface removes  Dipped in Fire,  Dipped in Poison, and  Burning on the weapon, if applicable.
  • Standing on a Water Surface cleans off dirt and blood, as does using these items: Soap Bar, Sponge, Sponge Tray, and Mattis's Holy Hand Soap
  • Water of sufficient depth (i.e. Deep Water) is considered Difficult Terrain in game.
