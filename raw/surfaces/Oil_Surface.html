This article is about the slick surface that can be ignited. For other uses, see Oil (disambiguation).

Oil is a type of surface. Oil surfaces have no effects while stepping on them, however will ignite and turn into a Fire surface when in contact with flame, such as from a Fireball spell or a lit Candle.

Description

Flammable.

Properties

Type: Surface

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

  • Oil Barrel
  • Oil Flask

Interactions

  • Turns into a  Fire surface when hit with Fire damage.
