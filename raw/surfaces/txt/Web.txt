This article is about the spell. For other uses, see Web (disambiguation). 

Web is a level 2 conjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to conjure a mass of sticky and flammable web at an area within range. Creatures moving or entering this area have their speed halved and can become caught in the web.

 Description 

Cover an area in thick, flammable webbing that can  Enweb creatures within.

An ally can use its  Help action to try and tear away the webs.

 How to learn 

Classes: 
- Class level 3: Sorcerer, Wizard, and Circle of the Land (Underdark)
- Class level 5: Swarmkeeper
- Class level 6: College of Lore (via Magical Secrets)
- Class level 8: Arcane Trickster and Eldritch Knight
- Class level 10: Bard (via Magical Secrets)

Other ways to learn:

- Wizards with  Level 2 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Web to their spellbooks.

 Notes 

- A similar ability is also accessible to Beast Master Rangers through  Wolf Spider Companion, and all Druids through  Wild Shape: Spider.

- Jumping into a web negates a portion of any falling damage and, possibly, the  Prone effect from falling from a height of 8 m (27 ft) or higher.

- The incantation for Web is Voco Araniae, Latin for "I call/summon cobwebs".
