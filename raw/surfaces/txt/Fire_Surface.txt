Fire (surface)

	

 
From bg3.wiki

 

(Redirected from Fire Surface)

 

 
 

 Jump to navigation
 Jump to search
 

Fire is a type of surface. When stepped on, characters catch on fire and take Fire damage every round. Dipping weapons into this surface makes them deal an additional 1d4 Fire damage.

Description

Deals 1d4⁠⁠Fire damage per turn.

Properties

 Type: Surface
 Dippable

 Condition: Burning 

 Burning
 Duration: 1 turn

- Takes 1d4⁠⁠Fire damage per turn.

- Can be removed with the  Help action, using a Healing Potion or gaining Wet.

- Immune from if Wet.

-  Dip action can be used on Burning characters or objects.

 

 

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

- Alchemist's Fire

- Arrow of Fire

- Concussion Grenade

- Flammable Slime Bomb

- Runepowder Vial

- Runepowder Barrel

Spells and actions:

- Chromatic Orb: Fire

- Death Burst (Fire)

- Erupting Cinder

- Fiery Regurgitation

- Firewine Explosion

- Incinerate

- Self Immolation

- Smouldering Touch

- Sphere of Elemental Balance: Fire

Interactions

 Dippable

A Fire surface can also be created by:

- Exploding a Oil, Smokepowder and/or Firewine Barrel.

- Igniting an Oil, Smokepowder, Grease, Unstable Blood, and/or Alcohol.

When the fire burns out, it leaves behind an  Ash surface.

- Turns into a  Steam Cloud for Duration: 10 turns after being exposed to water surface.
