Create Water is a level 1 transmutation spell. This spell is a variant of  Create or Destroy Water that allows spellcasters to conjure an area of water that rain from thin air and forms a water surface. Creatures standing in the area when the spell is cast will become  Wet.

 Description 

Call forth rain. It extinguishes exposed flames and forms a  Water surface.
 Properties 

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: 9 m (30 ft)
 AoE: 4 m (13 ft) Radius
 Creates Area: Water

 At higher levels 

Upcasting: When the spell is cast a 2nd Level or higher, the area that you can create water increases by 2 m (7 ft) for each spell slot level above 1st.

 How to learn

Create or Destroy Water

 Notes 

- Any invisible characters caught in the area of effect will be revealed, with no saving throw.

- The incantations for Create Water are Pluo ("Rain") and Aqua pura ("Pure water").

- A hidden character that is out-of-combat can cast this spell on in-combat characters without using an action. While casting the spell will unhide you and likely force you into combat, the character who Created this water will still have an action to use in the first turn of combat, as though they hadn't cast a spell.
