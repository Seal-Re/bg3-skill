Acid is a surface that decreases Armour Class by 2 when stepped on.

Description

Reduces  Armour Class by 2.

Properties

 Type: Surface

 Condition: Acid 

 Acid
 Duration: Whilst on surface

- Reduces  Armour Class by 2.

 

 

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

- Acid Barrel

- Acid Vial

- Arrow of Acid

Spells and actions:

- Acidic Regurgitation

- Chromatic Orb: Acid

- Corrosive Phlegm

- Corrosive Strike

- Melf's Acid Arrow

- Ochre Sputum (acid)

- Sphere of Elemental Balance: Acid

Interactions

- Acid surfaces created on top of  Blood surfaces will not apply the  Acid condition to creatures.
