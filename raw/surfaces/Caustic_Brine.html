Caustic Brine

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Caustic Brine is a type of surface created by throwing or breaking a Caustic Bulb. It also appears after destroying some environmental clutter found on Nautiloid such as Brine Bulb and Brain Jar.

Description

A pungent odour and acidic heat rise from this peculiar liquid. Deals 1d4⁠⁠Acid damage per turn.

Properties

Type: Surface

Condition: Caustic Brine

 Caustic Brine

Duration: Whilst on surface

  • Takes 1d4⁠⁠Acid damage per turn.

Creation

This area can be created by the following:

Items:

  • Brine Barrel
  • Caustic Bulb

Spells and actions:

  • Morphic Brine Explosion

Interactions

Creatures stepping on the surface suffer:

  • 1d4⁠⁠Acid damage (no  Saving Throw)

Can be turned into a  Fire surface, which causes a harmless explosion if hit with Fire or exposed to a Burning object and / or character.

Potions dropped (not thrown) on the caustic brine will shatter instantly but their effects will still remain on the ground.
