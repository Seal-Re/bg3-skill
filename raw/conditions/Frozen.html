Frozen

  • Affected entity is utterly enamelled in ice and is  Incapacitated. If it is dealt Bludgeoning, Thunder, or Force damage, the ice shatters, ending the condition.
  • Gains Vulnerability to ⁠Bludgeoning, ⁠Thunder, and ⁠Force damage.
  • Gains Resistance to ⁠Fire damage.
  • Gains immunity to  Burning and  Encrusted with Frost.

 Incapacitated

Properties

 Status groups: SG_Condition, SG_Incapacitated

 More properties:
  • InitiateCombat
  • OverheadOnTurn

Notes

  • Can be inflicted by making the target  Chilled then  Wet, replacing both conditions. Applying the conditions in the other order does not work.
  • The vulnerabilities are not mentioned on the in-game tooltip.

Sources of Frozen

  • Chilled
  • Encrusted with Frost
