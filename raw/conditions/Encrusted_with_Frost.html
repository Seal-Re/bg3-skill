Encrusted with Frost (Condition)

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Encrusted with Frost

  • Affected entity has  Disadvantage on Dexterity  Saving Throws.
  • When there are 7 or more turns remaining, the entity must succeed a  DC 12 Constitution Saving Throw or take 1d4⁠⁠ColdDRS and become  Frozen. On a successful save, it only takes half damage. Afterward, the frost sloughs away.
  • Removed by  Burning.

Properties

 Stack ID: MAG_FROST

 Loses duration: start of turn

 If already applied: add to duration

Condition: Frozen

 Frozen

Duration: 2 turns

 CON Save (DC 12)

  • Affected entity is utterly enamelled in ice and is  Incapacitated. If it is dealt Bludgeoning, Thunder, or Force damage, the ice shatters, ending the condition.
  • Gains Vulnerability to ⁠Bludgeoning, ⁠Thunder, and ⁠Force damage.
  • Gains Resistance to ⁠Fire damage.
  • Gains immunity to  Burning and  Encrusted with Frost.

Notes

  •  Markoheshkir's Encrusted with Frost condition comes from the  Frost of Dark Winter variant of Kereska's Favour.
  • Testing as of Patch 7 confirms that the save is fixed DC 12, despite the combat log showing a variable DC calculation involving Proficiency and Spellcasting Ability Modifier bonuses.

Bugs

  • The in-game description is not entirely correct: failing the  DC 12 Constitution Saving Throw results in the entity becoming  Frozen, whereas succeeding it causes it to take 1d4⁠⁠Cold damage. Contrary to what the description states, this damage is not halved.

Sources of Encrusted with Frost

  • Coated in Oil of Freezing
  • Conduit of Kereska's Ice
  • Oil of Freezing
  •  Coldbrim Chill
  •  Winter's Clutches
