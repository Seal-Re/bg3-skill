Brittle

  • Affected entity has been rapidly cooled while aflame, making it Vulnerable to Thunder and Bludgeoning damage.
  • Takes 2d6⁠⁠Cold damage at the start of their turn.

Properties

 Stack ID: BRITTLE

 Loses duration: start of turn
