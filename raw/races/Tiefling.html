Tiefling

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Tieflings are a playable race in Baldur's Gate 3. Touched by the Hells, tieflings are resistant to fire and carry extraordinary Infernal powers.

“
Descended from devils of the Nine Hells, tieflings face constant suspicion in Faerûn. Thankfully, their arcane abilities make them natural survivors.
„
— In-game racial description

About tieflings[edit section | visual editor]

Tieflings are mortals marked by infernal blood tracing back to the Nine Hells. Born from ancient pacts or distant devilish ancestry, they bear horns, tails, and ember-bright eyes that set them apart. Across Faerûn, they frequently face suspicion, prejudice, and fear, regardless of how they choose to live.[1] Whether descended from Asmodeus, Mephistopheles, or Zariel, many tieflings by necessity develop a resilient, self-reliant nature as they continually strive to define themselves on their own terms – as survivors, mercenaries, or heroes in an often unwelcoming world.[2]

Racial features[edit section | visual editor]

Base Racial Speed
You can move 9 m (30 ft) per turn.

Can see in the dark up to 12 m (40 ft).
Hellish Resistance
Your blood protects you from flame, abyssal or otherwise. Gain Resistance to ⁠Fire damage, taking only half damage from it.

Subraces[edit section | visual editor]

Asmodeus tiefling[edit section | visual editor]

“
Bound to Nessus, the deepest layer of the Hells, these tieflings inherited the ability to wield fire and darkness from the archdevil Asmodeus' infernal bloodline.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace Features[edit section | visual editor]

  • Infernal Legacy
Gain the following Spells:
  • Cantrip:  Produce Flame (Level 1).
  • 1st Level Spell:  Infernal Legacy: Hellish Rebuke (Level 3; casts as if using a second-level slot;  Recharge: Long rest).
  • 2nd Level Spell:  Darkness (Level 5;  Recharge: Long rest).
Note: These spells use Charisma as their spellcasting modifier, except for  Produce Flame.[note 1]

Mephistopheles tiefling[edit section | visual editor]

“
Descended from the archdevil Mephistopheles, these tieflings are gifted with a particular affinity for arcane magic.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace Features[edit section | visual editor]

  • Legacy of Cania
Gain the following Spells:
  • Cantrip:  Mage Hand (Level 1).
  • 1st level spell:  Legacy of Cania: Burning Hands (Level 3; casts as if using a second-level slot;  Recharge: Long rest).
  • 2nd level spell:  Legacy of Cania: Flame Blade (Level 5;  Recharge: Long rest).
Note: These spells use Charisma as their spellcasting modifier.

Zariel tiefling[edit section | visual editor]

“
Tieflings from Zariel's bloodline are empowered with martial strength, and can channel searing flame to punish their enemies.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace Features[edit section | visual editor]

  • Legacy of Avernus
Gain the following Spells:
  • Cantrip:  Thaumaturgy (Level 1).
  • 1st Level Spell:  Legacy of Avernus: Searing Smite (Level 3; casts as if using a second-level slot;  Recharge: Long rest).
  • 2nd Level Spell:  Legacy of Avernus: Branding Smite (Level 5;  Recharge: Long rest).
Note: These spells use Charisma as their spellcasting modifier.
