Elf

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Elves are a playable race. They are long lived, and have an affinity for magic.

“
With ethereal countenances and long lifespans, elves are at home with nature's power, flourishing in light and dark alike.
„
— In-game racial description

About elves[edit section | visual editor]

Elves are an ancient and long-lived people, their origins entwined with the  Feywild and the primal magic of the world. Possessed of keen senses and unearthly grace, they move through life with a patience born of centuries. Elven societies prize art, poetry, and arcane study, preserving traditions that stretch back to lost empires and forgotten ages. To other races, their measured speech and distant composure can seem aloof, yet beneath it lies deep feeling and fierce loyalty.

Across Faerûn, elven cultures differ — from reclusive woodland kin who guard ancestral forests to cosmopolitan high elves devoted to scholarship and spellcraft. Though diminished from their ancient heights, elves remain bound to memory and legacy, carrying the weight of history even as they carve careful paths into the future.

Racial features[edit section | visual editor]

Base Racial Speed
You can move 9 m (30 ft) per turn.
Size
Medium
Keen Senses
You gain Proficiency in the  Perception skill.
Elven Weapon Training
You have Proficiency with  Longswords,  Shortswords,  Longbows, and  Shortbows.
Darkvision
Can see in the dark up to 12 m (40 ft).
Fey Ancestry
The Feywild casts a veil over your mind. You have  Advantage on  Saving Throws against being  Charmed, and magic can't put you to  Sleep.
