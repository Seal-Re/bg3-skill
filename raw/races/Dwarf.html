Dwarves are a playable race in Baldur's Gate 3.

“
As durable and unyielding as their homes of stone, dwarves are some of the finest warriors, miners, and smiths of Faerûn.
„
— In-game racial description

About dwarves[edit section | visual editor]

Dwarves are a stalwart and ancient people, shaped by stone, steel, and unyielding tradition. Dwelling in mountain halls and subterranean strongholds, they hold craftsmanship, clan loyalty, and ancestral honor as central pillars of their culture.[1] Their lives are long, their memories longer still, and every triumph or grievance is etched into song and stone.[2] Patience and discipline guide them, whether at the forge, the council table, or the battlefield.

Dwarven culture is diverse across Faerûn. Shield dwarves and gold dwarves fiercely uphold the legacies of their clans and mountain kingdoms, while duergar – the gray dwarves of the Underdark – embody a harsher existence forged through ancient enslavement by illithids, relying on cunning, secrecy, and iron will.[3] Across Faerûn, dwarves are respected for their skill, resilience, and loyalty – remaining steadfast allies and formidable foes.

NPC characters[edit section | visual editor]

Additionally, non-player duergar characters also have:

Sunlight Sensitivity

Religions[edit section | visual editor]

The church of Moradin[edit section | visual editor]

The Dwarffather inspires dwarven inventions and is constantly seeking to improve his race, encouraging a good-natured, intelligent, and harmonious existence with other good races while battling their pride and isolationist tendencies.

Worshipers of Laduguer[edit section | visual editor]

His clerics are the unquestioned rulers of duergar society, enforcing cold and callous behaviour among his worshippers. Laduguer is a xenophobic god with a doctrine of empowerment through slave exploitation and protection through an unforgiving military program. He is at odds with the other dwarven god Moradin.

Cleric deities[edit section | visual editor]

Due to the position Laduguer takes within duergar society, only duergar  Clerics are able to pick him as deity.

Equipment[edit section | visual editor]

Certain items have unique bonuses when wielded or worn by a Dwarf. These bonuses can be enabled by being  disguised as one before equipping the item.

  • Dwarven Thrower: Dwarven Thrower (passive feature) - a Dwarf throwing this warhammer deals an extra 1d8⁠⁠Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large, Huge or Gargantuan creature, the strike deals an additional 2d8⁠⁠Bludgeoning damage.
  • Nimblefinger Gloves: Nimblefinger - a Dwarf wearing this gloves gains +1 Dexterity.

Notes[edit section | visual editor]

  • Each Dwarf subrace equates to a specific type of Dwarf subrace in Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition, as they share their unique traits:
    • A Gold Dwarf equates to a Hill Dwarf in 5e
    • A Shield Dwarf equates to a Mountain Dwarf in 5e.

Bugs[edit section | visual editor]

  • Dwarves are subject to bugs resulting from errors in animation scripting:
    • Due to a timing error, reactions such as  Deflect Missiles cannot be used against Dwarves attacking with two-handed crossbows as the attack animation finishes before the reaction can trigger.
    • Due to erroneous animation binding of  Off-Hand Melee Attacks initiated from outside melee range, i.e., 1.5 m (5 ft), male Dwarves perform two off-hand attacks from a single off-hand melee attack (1  Bonus Action) when a specific combination of weapons is equipped:
      • The main-hand weapon must be Small1H (e.g., a Dagger)
      • The off-hand weapon must be Piercing1H (e.g., a Shortsword)
