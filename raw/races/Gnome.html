Gnomes are a playable race in Baldur's Gate 3.

“
Small, clever, and energetic, gnomes use their long lives to explore Faerûn's brightest corners and darkest depths.
„
— In-game racial description

About gnomes[edit section | visual editor]

Gnomes are a small, long-lived race known for their sharp intellect, innate magic, and boundless curiosity. Distantly related to the fey,[1] their keen minds and natural mental resilience – known as Gnome Cunning[2] – allow them to parse complex arcane theory, master intricate engineering, or navigate danger with cleverness rather than brute force. Though many gnomes approach life with cheerful optimism and a delight in discovery, their culture is far from monolithic. In Faerûn, gnomes span a wide spectrum: from the inventive Gondian artificers of Baldur's Gate to the reclusive deep gnomes of the Underdark. Whether crafting bizarre contraptions, unlocking ancient lore, or fighting for survival in shadowy subterranean realms, gnomes rely on resourcefulness and ingenuity to leave an indelible mark on the world.

Size
Small
Gnome Cunning
You have  Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma Saving Throws.
Shortened Stride
Your movement speed is 7.5 m (25 ft).

Subraces[edit section | visual editor]

Rock gnome[edit section | visual editor]

“
The most commonly seen gnomes on Faerûn's surface, rock gnomes are named as such for their hardiness and affinity for metal.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace features[edit section | visual editor]

Darkvision
Can see in the dark up to 12 m (40 ft).
Artificer's Lore
Add twice your  Proficiency Bonus to  History checks.

Forest gnome[edit section | visual editor]

“
Even smaller than their cousins and twice as reclusive, forest gnomes are a rare sight in Faerûn. They master magic and craftsmanship in their distant, idyllic groves.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace features[edit section | visual editor]

Darkvision
Can see in the dark up to 12 m (40 ft).
 Speak with Animals
Gain the ability to comprehend and communicate with beasts.  Recharge: Long rest.

Deep gnome[edit section | visual editor]

“
More guarded than their surface cousins, deep gnomes survive in the Underdark with darkvision and skilful stealth.
„
— In-game racial description

Subrace features[edit section | visual editor]

Superior Darkvision
Can see in the dark up to 24 m (80 ft).
Stone Camouflage
You have  Advantage on  Stealth checks.

Equipment[edit section | visual editor]

Certain items have unique bonuses when wielded or worn by a Gnome. These bonuses can be enabled by being  disguised as one before equipping the item.

  • Nimblefinger Gloves: Nimblefinger - a Gnome wearing this gloves gains +2 Dexterity.

Rock gnomes uniquely start with a  Music Box.
