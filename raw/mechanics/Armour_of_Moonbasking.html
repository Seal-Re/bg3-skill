Armour of Moonbasking

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Armour of Moonbasking is a very rare Light Armour that bestows Temporary Hit Points and Armour Class to its wielder when they Wild Shape.

This armour was designed by lunar-obsessed Cuthbertina, a gnomish arcane smith who now lives peacefully on a mountain plateau with her magical forge.

Special

The wearer of this item gains:

Lunar Bestial Vitality
You gain  22 temporary hit points after casting  Wild Shape. While those temporary hit points are active reduce all incoming damage by 1.
Lunar Bestial Fortitude
You have a +2 bonus to  Armour Class. You also have  Advantage on  Saving Throws against spells. This effect persists while using your druidic  Wild Shape ability.

Where to find

  • Lower City Sewers X: -157 Y: 939: Sold by  Voiceless Penitent Bareki

Notes

  • This armour is largely intended for a Circle of the Moon and Circle of the Stars druids. It is one of three light armours designed for specific druid subclasses, the others being  Armour of Landfall for Circle of the Land and  Armour of the Sporekeeper for Circle of Spores.

Regarding Lunar Bestial Vitality:

  • Circle of the Stars  Starry Form counts as  Wild Shape for the purposes of Lunar Bestial Vitality.
  • Speaking to a party member in  Wild Shape and then ending the conversation briefly undoes the transformation, thus causing the temporary hit points to be refreshed.

Regarding Lunar Bestial Fortitude:

  • Circle of the Stars  Starry Form counts as  Wild Shape for the purposes of Lunar Bestial Fortitude.

Bugs

Regarding Lunar Bestial Vitality:

  • Switching Starry Forms with  Twinkling Constellations, or dismissing a disguise from  Disguise Self while affected by a Starry Form, removes the temporary hit points from  Lunar Vitality. The AC and saving throw bonus from Lunar Protection remains.

Regarding Lunar Bestial Fortitude:

  •  Lunar Protection granted by Lunar Bestial Fortitude incorrectly states it provides a +1d4 bonus to Armour Class; it is a flat +2 bonus.
  • Lunar Protection persists on the character after unequipping Armour of Moonbasking while affected by Starry Form.
  • When affected by a Starry Form, the bonus AC is applied a second time, resulting in a total AC bonus of 4.
