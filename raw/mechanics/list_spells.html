Cantrips[edit section | visual editor]

Cantrips are level 0 spells which do not cost Spell Slots to cast. They cannot be upcast, but most damage-dealing cantrips gain an additional damage die - typically at character levels 5 and 10 respectively.

  • Acid Splash
  • Blade Ward
  • Bone Chill
  • Booming Blade
  • Bursting Sinew
