Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Sorcerers use their innate magical powers to fight enemies and aid allies in combat. Sorcerers use Charisma as their primary ability score.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Sorcerer subclasses available in the game are:

  •  Draconic Bloodline
  •  Shadow Magic
  •  Storm Sorcery
  •  Wild Magic

Class Information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At level 1: 6 +  Constitution modifier
On level up: 4 +  Constitution modifier
Key abilities
 Charisma for Spells
 Constitution for Hit Points and  Concentration Checks
 Dexterity for Armour Class
Spellcasting Ability
 Charisma
Known Spells
For a list of spells available to the Sorcerer, see: List of Sorcerer spells.
Sorcerer Spells are Always Prepared. In exchange, Sorcerers know fewer spells than Wizards.

Starting Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

Saving throw proficiencies
 Constitution,  Charisma
Equipment proficiencies
 Daggers,  Quarterstaves,  Light Crossbows
Skills with proficiency (Choose 2)
 Arcana,  Deception,  Insight,  Intimidation,  Persuasion,  Religion
You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
Starting Equipment
 Quarterstaff,  Sorcerer Robe (variant depends on subclass),  Simple Boots

Multiclass Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

No additional proficiencies are granted when multiclassing into Sorcerer.

 Sorcery Points: 2
 Recharge: Long rest
Sorcery Points are the innate power of sorcerers. They can use these points to alter Spells or gain Spell Slots. You gain 2 Sorcery Points.
 Create Spell Slot ( + 2 )
Spend Sorcery Points to unlock a Spell Slot.
 Create Sorcery Points ( + )
Spend Spell Slots to gain Sorcery Points. The level of spell slot spent creates the equivalent number of  Sorcery Points.
Metamagic
Choose 2 Metamagic:
Metamagic  Sorcery Points Grants
Careful Spell 1 Allies automatically succeed Saving Throws against spells that require them.
Distant Spell 1 Extends the range of a spell by 50%. Melee spells are increased to  Range: 9 m (30 ft).
Extended Spell 1 Double the duration of Conditions, summons, and surfaces caused by spells.
Twinned Spell 1 per Spell Slot level (Cantrips are 1) Spells that only target 1 creature can target an additional creature.
