Controlled Chaos

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Controlled Chaos is a Wild Magic Sorcerer reaction that triggers a Wild Magic effect after an enemy spellcaster casts a spell.

Description

Foes may suffer a Wild Magic Surge while casting spells near your fluctuating magic.

Properties

Cost
 Reaction

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 11: Wild Magic Sorcerer

Notes

  • There is no range restriction for this reaction despite what the description suggests.
  • The surges triggered by this reaction are chosen from a smaller list consisting predominately of the harmful effects.
