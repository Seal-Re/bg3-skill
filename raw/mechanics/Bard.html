Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Bards are expert storytellers, artists, and performers. Bards channel their power through song, speech, or performance to cast Spells. Bards use Charisma as their primary ability score.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Bard Subclasses are:

  •  College of Glamour
  •  College of Lore
  •  College of Swords
  •  College of Valour

Class information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At level 1: 8 +  Constitution modifier
On level up: 5 +  Constitution modifier
Key Ability Scores
 Charisma for improved spell's DC and Attack Rolls with spells.
 Constitution for more Hit Points and better  Concentration checks.
 Dexterity for higher Initiative and Armour Class.
Spellcasting Ability
 Charisma
Known spells
For a list of spells available to the Bard, see: List of Bard spells.
Bard Spells are Always Prepared. In exchange, Bards know fewer Spells at low levels.

Starting Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

Saving Throw proficiencies
 Dexterity,  Charisma
Equipment proficiencies
Simple weapons,  Hand Crossbows,  Rapiers,  Longswords,  Shortswords
 Light armour
Skills with proficiency (Choose 3)
 History,  Insight,  Medicine,  Persuasion,  Religion,  Deception,  Intimidation,  Performance,  Acrobatics,  Sleight of Hand,  Stealth,  Animal Handling,  Arcana,  Investigation,  Athletics,  Nature,  Perception,  Survival
You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
Starting Equipment
 Hand Crossbow,  Simple Jerkin,  Leather Boots, an Instrument chosen during character creation
