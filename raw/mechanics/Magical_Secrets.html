Magical Secrets

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Magical Secrets is a level 6 College of Lore, and level 10 base Bard class feature in which they learn 2 Spells from a set belonging to other classes.

Description[edit section | visual editor]

Your versatile knack for the arcane lets you learn magic from every discipline. Choose some additional spells to add to your repertoire.

Available Spells[edit section | visual editor]

At level 6, College of Lore Bards can learn two spells up to level 3. At level 10, all Bards can learn two spells up to level 5.
