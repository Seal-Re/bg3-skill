Divine Smite

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
This article is about the action to perform a Divine Smite. For the reaction to apply a Divine Smite after making an attack, see Divine Smite (reaction). For general information about smites, see Smite.
Divine Smite is a spell-like ability that allows a Paladin to expend a spell slot to deal additional ⁠Radiant damage with a melee weapon attack.

Description

Your weapon deals an additional 2d8⁠⁠RadiantDRS damage, increased by 1d8⁠⁠Radiant damage when attacking Fiends or Undead.

Properties

Cost
 Action
Cost on hit
 Level 1 Spell Slot
Damage: 3~24
Normal weapon damage
+ 2d8⁠Radiant
+ 1d8⁠Radiant (If the target is undead or a fiend)
Details
 Melee Weapon Attack Roll
 Range: Normal weapon range

At higher levels

  • When Divine Smite is cast at 2nd level or higher, damage increases by 1d8⁠⁠Radiant for each spell slot level above 1st. This damage is capped to 5d8, so using a spell slot above 4th spell level adds no further damage.

Bugs

  • The  Level 1 Spell Slot version of this action changes attack animations in Honour mode due to an override in the code.
  • The  Level 4 Spell Slot,  Level 5 Spell Slot, and  Level 6 Spell Slot versions of this action do not lose DRS in Honour mode when used as an attack, while the equivalent reaction versions do.
