## Clouds

From bg3.wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search

Clouds are a type of area. Clouds hang directly above the ground, as opposed to surfaces that coat the ground itself.

## Properties[edit section | visual editor]

Clouds generally apply their effects when a character enters their zone of influence. Beyond this, most clouds are more specific and individualized in their effects. Some clouds apply an additional effect when beginning or ending a turn within the cloud; some clouds' effects only persist while within the cloud, while others apply conditions that persist after leaving. 

Clouds can be removed by 
 Gust of Wind and 
 Rush of the Gale Spirits.

Unlike surfaces, clouds are not dippable.
