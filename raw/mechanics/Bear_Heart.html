Bear Heart

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Bear Heart is a passive feature that grants a Wildheart Barbarian a special version of Rage and another ability.

Description

While  Raging, you can use  Unrelenting Ferocity, and have Resistance to all damage except Psychic damage.

Grants:

  •  Rage: Bear Heart
  •  Unrelenting Ferocity
