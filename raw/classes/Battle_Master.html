Battle Master

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Battle Master is one of the subclasses of Fighter. They focus on using active skills on a limited resource to hinder and disable opponents as well as sometimes supporting allies.

“
As a paragon of tactical superiority, you employ a wide array of combat manoeuvres to dominate every fight.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Fighter, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

 Superiority Dice: 4
Your Superiority Dice are d8s. They fuel your Battle Master manoeuvres and are expended upon use.
You regain expended Superiority Dice after either a Short Rest or a Long Rest.
Manoeuvres
Choose 3 manoeuvres to learn

Level 7[edit section | visual editor]

 Superiority Dice: 5
You gain +1 Superiority Die
  • Choose 2 additional manoeuvres.

Level 10[edit section | visual editor]

Improved Combat Superiority
The size of your  Superiority Die increases to a d10
  • Choose 2 additional manoeuvres.

Manoeuvres[edit section | visual editor]

Manoeuvre save DC = 8 + Proficiency Modifier + your Strength or Dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

Except for  Riposte and  Sweeping Attack, manoeuvres that require an attack roll only consume a  Superiority Die if the attack hits.
