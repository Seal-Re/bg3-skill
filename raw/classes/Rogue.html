Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Rogues are well versed in the art of stealth and rely on their resourcefulness to be in control of any challenging situation. Rogues use Dexterity as their primary ability score.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Rogue Subclasses are:

  •  Arcane Trickster
  •  Assassin
  •  Swashbuckler
  •  Thief

Class information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At level 1: 8 +  Constitution modifier
On level up: 5 +  Constitution modifier
Key abilities
 Dexterity for physical attacks and Armour Class
 Constitution for Hit Points
 Intelligence for Spells (Arcane Trickster only)
 Charisma for "dirty trick" abilities (Swashbuckler only), (exception for sand toss)
Spellcasting Ability
 Intelligence for the effective use of items, scrolls, and equipment.

Starting Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

Saving throw proficiencies
 Dexterity,  Intelligence
Equipment proficiencies
Simple weapons,  Hand Crossbows,  Longswords,  Rapiers,  Shortswords
 Light armour
Skills with proficiency (Choose 4)
 Acrobatics,  Athletics,  Deception,  Insight,  Intimidation,  Investigation,  Perception,  Performance,  Persuasion,  Sleight of Hand,  Stealth
You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
Class Starting Equipment
 Shortsword,  Leather Armour (purple variant),  Leather Boots,  Thieves' Tools,  Grease Bottle x2

Multiclass Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

Equipment proficiencies
 Light armour
Skills with proficiency (Choose 1)
Same list as Starting Proficiencies

Level progression[edit section | visual editor]

Through experience, you acquire new power. You gain the following traits.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

  • Gain  Expertise in 2 Skills you are Proficient in.
Sneak Attack
 Sneak Attack (Melee) ()
Deal an extra 1d6 damage to a foe you have  Advantage against. ( Recharge: Per turn.)
 Sneak Attack (Ranged) ()
Deal an extra 1d6 damage to a foe you have  Advantage against. ( Recharge: Per turn.)

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

Cunning Action
 Cunning Action: Dash ()
Cover more distance this turn: double your movement speed.
 Cunning Action: Disengage ()
Retreat safely. Moving won't provoke Opportunity Attacks.
 Cunning Action: Hide ()
Hide from enemies by succeeding at  Stealth checks. Stick to the dark and avoid enemy sightlines. Attacking or casting a spell will reveal your location.

Your agility lets you dodge out of the way of certain spells. When a spell or effect would deal half damage on a successful  Dexterity Saving Throw, it deals no damage if you succeed, and only half damage if you fail.
