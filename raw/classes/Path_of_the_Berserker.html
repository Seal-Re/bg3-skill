Berserker

From bg3.wiki
(Redirected from Path of the Berserker)
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Berserker is one of the subclasses of Barbarian. These Barbarians are more specialized in using their anger to gain more abilities while raging in combat.

“
Violence is both a means and an end. You follow a path of untrammelled fury, slick with blood, as you thrill in the chaos of battle, heedless of your own well-being.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Barbarian, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

Rage Becomes Frenzy
Your bloodthirst transforms your rage, making it stronger.


 Frenzy ( + )
Your  Rage turns into a frenzy! You gain  Frenzied Strike and  Enraged Throw. You can also make an  Improvised Melee Weapon attack as a bonus action.
 Frenzied Strike ()
As a bonus action, make a melee attack with your equipped weapon. ( Recharge: Per turn.)
 Enraged Throw ()
Pick up an item or creature and throw it at a target, dealing additional damage and knocking it  Prone.

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Mindless Rage
Your rage becomes all-consuming, repelling outside influence. While  Frenzied, you can't be  Charmed or  Frightened, and  Calm Emotions no longer ends your rage.

Level 10[edit section | visual editor]

 Intimidating Presence ()
Menace an enemy and instil a terrible  Fear within them.
You can use  Maintain Intimidating Presence to prolong the target's fear.

Notes[edit section | visual editor]

  • The passive feature Rage Becomes Frenzy is not an available (bonus) action to select. Instead, it refers to a Berserker's ability to automatically enter a state of Frenzy, instead of Rage. Barbarians with the Berserker subclass have this passive listed under the Character Summary of Notable Features.
