Draconic Bloodline

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Draconic Bloodline is one of the subclasses of Sorcerer. These Sorcerers are harder to hit than their counterparts. They also have the ability to gain resistance and deal more damage with certain types of damage based on their bloodline.

“
Your veins carry draconic magic, the result of a powerful dragon ancestor.
„

Subclass features[edit section | visual editor]

This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Sorcerer, in addition to its unique features outlined below.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

Draconic Resilience
Dragon-like scales cover parts of your skin. When you aren't wearing armour, your base  Armour Class is 13.

This bonus does not stack with Mage Armour. You may also choose an alternate appearance.

Draconic Resilience: Hit Points
Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1 for each Sorcerer level.

Level 6[edit section | visual editor]

Elemental Affinity: Damage
When you cast a spell that deals damage of the type associated with your draconic ancestry, you add your Charisma Modifier to the damage.
 Elemental Affinity: Resistance ()
When you cast a spell that deals damage matching your draconic ancestry, you can spend a  Sorcery Point to gain Resistance to that damage type.

Level 11[edit section | visual editor]

 Fly ()
Fly to a target position

Appearance[edit section | visual editor]

The colour of the dragon scales is determined by the character's chosen Draconic Ancestry. It is possible however to change the scale colour to any other using the Magic Mirror at camp or during character creation.

  • Default Red Ancestry
  • Default Black Ancestry
  • Default Blue Ancestry
  • Default White Ancestry
  • Default Green Ancestry
  • Default Gold Ancestry
  • Default Silver Ancestry
  • Default Bronze Ancestry
  • Default Copper Ancestry
  • Default Brass Ancestry

Dragonborn sorcerers get special coloured scales all over their bodies instead of only their face. The colour depends on the chosen Draconic Ancestry. It is also possible to change these colours to any other while editing their appearance, including during character creation.

  • Red Ancestry on a White Dragonborn
