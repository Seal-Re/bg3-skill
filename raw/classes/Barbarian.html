Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Barbarians use their martial prowess and primal rage to strengthen themselves and dominate enemies in combat.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Barbarian Subclasses are:

  •  Berserker
  •  Giant
  •  Wild Magic
  •  Wildheart

Class information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At level 1: 12 +  Constitution modifier
On level up: 7 +  Constitution modifier
Key abilities
 Strength for improved attack rolls and damage with melee weapons.
 Dexterity for higher initiative and Armour Class
 Constitution for more hit points and unarmoured Armour Class.
Spellcasting Ability
 Charisma for the effective use of actions, items, scrolls, and equipment.

Level progression[edit section | visual editor]

Through experience, you acquire new power. You gain the following traits.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

 Rage Charges: 2
The number of times you can Rage per day. Replenished by long rest.
 Rage ( + )
Deal 2 extra damage with melee and improvised weapons, and throwing.
Gain Resistance to physical damage, and  Advantage on Strength Checks and Saving Throws.
Unarmoured Defence
Your body is as resilient as any armour. While not wearing armour, you add your Constitution Modifier to your  Armour Class.

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

 Reckless Attack ()
Throw all caution in the wind to gain  Advantage on your melee  Attack Roll. Until your next turn, you have advantage on attack rolls, but enemies also have advantage on attack rolls against you.
Danger Sense
You sense when things aren't as they should be. You have  Advantage on  Dexterity Saving Throws, such as against traps, spells, and surfaces. To gain this benefit, you can't be  Blinded or  Incapacitated.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

 Rage Charges: 3
Gain an additional Rage Charge. You can now Rage more times per long rest.
