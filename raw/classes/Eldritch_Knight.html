Eldritch Knight

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Eldritch Knight is one of the Subclasses of Fighter. These Fighters specialize in spellcasting, using their martial prowess and magic to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Eldritch Knights use Intelligence as their Spellcasting Ability, and learn spells from the Wizard spell list, favouring the schools of Evocation and Abjuration.

“
You are a warrior of spell and sword, weaving incantations that supplement your extensive martial expertise.
„
