Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Fighters use their martial expertise to fight enemies and support their allies in combat. Fighters use Strength or Dexterity as their primary ability score.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Fighter subclasses are:

  •  Arcane Archer
  •  Battle Master
  •  Champion
  •  Eldritch Knight

Class information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At Level 1: 10 +  Constitution Modifier
On level up: 6 +  Constitution Modifier
Key Ability Scores
 Strength for physical attacks.
 Dexterity for physical attacks with Finesse and Ranged weapons, Armour Class (non- Heavy armour), and Initiative.
 Constitution for Hit Points (and  Concentration Checks for Eldritch Knights).
 Intelligence for Spells (Eldritch Knight and Arcane Archer).
Spellcasting Ability
 Intelligence for the effective use of items, scrolls, and equipment.

Starting Proficiencies[edit section | visual editor]

Saving throw proficiencies
 Strength,  Constitution
Equipment Proficiencies
Simple weapons, Martial weapons
 Light armour,  Medium armour,  Heavy armour
 Shields
Skills proficiencies (Choose 2)
 Acrobatics,  Animal Handling,  Athletics,  History,  Insight,  Intimidation,  Perception,  Survival
You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
Starting Equipment
 Longsword,  Scale Mail,  Leather Boots

Level progression[edit section | visual editor]

Through experience, you acquire new power. You gain the following traits.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

 Second Wind ()
Draw on your stamina to protect yourself. You regain 1d10 + Fighter Level⁠⁠Hit Points. ( Recharge: Short rest.)
Fighting Style
Choose a fighting style:
Archery
You gain a +2 bonus to  Attack Rolls you make with ranged Weapons.
Defence
You gain a +1 bonus to  Armour Class while wearing armour.
Duelling
When you are wielding a melee weapon that is not Two-Handed in one hand, and no weapon in the other, you deal an additional 2 damage with that weapon.
Great Weapon Fighting
When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack with a Two-Handed melee weapon, that die is rerolled once.
Protection
When you have a Shield, impose  Disadvantage on an enemy who attacks one of your allies when you are within 1.5 m (5 ft). You must be able to see the enemy.
Two-Weapon Fighting
When you make an offhand attack, you can add your Ability Score Modifier to the damage of the attack.

 Action Surge
Immediately gain an extra  Action to use this turn. ( Recharge: Short rest.)
