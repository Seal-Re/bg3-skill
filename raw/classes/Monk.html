Overview[edit section | visual editor]

Monks are unarmed combatants capable of spending Ki Points to perform special abilities. Monk's key ability scores are Wisdom, Dexterity, and Constitution, and their core ability is Unarmed Strike.

Subclasses[edit section | visual editor]

The Monk Subclasses are:

  •  Way of the Drunken Master
  •  Way of the Four Elements
  •  Way of the Open Hand
  •  Way of Shadow

Class information[edit section | visual editor]

Attributes[edit section | visual editor]

Hit points
At level 1: 8 +  Constitution modifier
On level up: 5 +  Constitution modifier
Key abilities
 Dexterity for bonuses to attack and damage rolls of unarmed strikes and monk weapons as well as Ki saving throw DCs.
 Constitution for more Hit Points.
 Wisdom for improved Armour Class. For Way of the Four Elements abilities, also improves Ki attack rolls and saving throw DCs.

Monk weapons
Monk weapons are weapons the Monk has Proficiency with (from any source, including race and multiclassing), and have neither the Heavy nor Two-Handed properties. Versatile weapons count as Monk weapons even if they are wielded with both hands. Monks can use Dexterity for those weapons, even if they do not have the Finesse property. Some Monk abilities, such as  Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike, are limited to Monk weapons.
Spellcasting ability
 Wisdom for the effective use of items, scrolls, and equipment.
Most Monk features use the associated weapon ability (e.g., the better of Strength or Dexterity). However, Way of the Four Elements Monks use their spellcasting ability for their unique class features other than Fangs of the Fire Snake[1] and Water Whip.

Level progression[edit section | visual editor]

Through experience, you acquire new power. You gain the following traits.

Level 1[edit section | visual editor]

 Ki Points: 2
You can spend these points to fuel various Monk abilities. Ki abilities which incorporate an unarmed attack use the higher of Dexterity or Strength for attack rolls and save DCs. This includes all offensive abilities outside of the Way of the Four Elements subclass.
 Recharge: Short rest

Martial Arts[edit section | visual editor]

Martial Arts: Dextrous Attacks
Attacks with Monk weapons and unarmed attacks scale with your Dexterity instead of your Strength if your Dexterity is higher.
Martial Arts: Deft Strikes
Attacks with Monk Weapons and unarmed attacks deal 1d4⁠⁠Bludgeoning damage, unless their normal damage is higher.
Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike
After making an attack with a melee Monk weapon or while unarmed, you can make another unarmed attack as a bonus action.
Unarmoured Defence
Your reflexes are as effective as any armour. While not wearing armour, you add your Wisdom Modifier to your  Armour Class.
 Flurry of Blows ( + )
Punch twice in quick succession.

Level 2[edit section | visual editor]

 Ki Points: 3
You gain an additional Ki Point to fuel your monk techniques.
Unarmoured Movement
Your movement speed increases by 3 m (10 ft) while you are not wearing armour or using a shield.
 Patient Defence ( + )
Attack Rolls against you have  Disadvantage, and you have  Advantage on  Dexterity Saving Throws.

Step of the Wind[edit section | visual editor]

 Step of the Wind: Dash ( + )
Double your movement speed. Jump no longer requires a  Bonus Action.
 Step of the Wind: Disengage ( + )
Move without triggering Opportunity Attacks. Jump no longer requires a  Bonus Action.

Level 3[edit section | visual editor]

 Ki Points: 4
You gain an additional Ki Point to fuel your monk techniques.
Martial Arts: Deft Strikes
Attacks with Monk Weapons and unarmed attacks deal 1d6⁠⁠Bludgeoning (previously 1d4) damage, unless their normal damage is higher.
