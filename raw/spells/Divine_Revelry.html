Divine Revelry

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Divine Revelry is a level 9 enchantment spell. It allows Gale to create a firewine barrel and force several creatures to dance in place.

Description

Conjure forth a barrel of Shadowdark Ale from the Yawning Portal and spread an  Irrestible Dance across all nearby creatures.

Properties

Cost
 Action
Details
 Range: 12 m (40 ft)
 AoE: 3 m (10 ft) Radius

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Condition: Galerian Revelry

 Galerian Revelry

Duration: 1 turn

  • Compelled to shuffle and dance.
  • Affected entity can't move or take actions.  Attack Rolls against it have  Advantage, while its Attack Rolls and Dexterity  Saving Throws have  Disadvantage.

How to learn

Other ways to learn:

  • Only Gale can have this spell during some outcomes in the Epilogue
