Plant Growth

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
See also: Plant Growth (surface)
Plant Growth is a level 3 transmutation spell. It allows spellcasters to cause an area to expel weeds from the ground, quartering the movement speed of all creatures travelling through the area.

Description

Make weeds burst from the ground and smother the area. Creatures moving through the weeds have their Movement Speed quartered.
