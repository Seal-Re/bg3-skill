Guidance

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Guidance is a cantrip (Divination). This cantrip allows spellcasters to grant a temporary bonus to a creature's ability checks.

Description

The target gains +1d4 bonus to  Ability Checks.

Properties

Cost
 Action
Details
 Melee: 1.5 m (5 ft)
 Concentration

Technical details

UID
Target_Guidance
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsConcentration, IsMelee, IsSpell

Condition: Guidance

 Guidance

Duration: 10 turns

  • Gains a +1d4 bonus to  Ability Checks.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Cleric and Druid
  • Class level 2: Circle of the Stars
  • Class level 3: Warlock (via Pact of the Tome) and Arcane Archer
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Cleric
  •  Magic Initiate: Druid

Granted by items:

  • Silver Pendant

Other ways to learn:

  • There are no scrolls available for wizards to transcribe to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This is a staple cantrip, and can be used as often as needed to buff the recipient's odds of success with most any skill.
    • Those who cannot cast this cantrip natively (e.g., Rogues) may consider using the  Silver Pendant to do so.
  • The incantation for Guidance is Maior et Fortior, Latin for "bigger and stronger".
