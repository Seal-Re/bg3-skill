Create Undead

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Create Undead is a level 6 necromancy spell. It allows spellcasters to animate a corpse, creating an undead servant.

Description

Create a corpse as a heinous mummy that fights by your side.

The target must be a Medium or Small corpse.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 6 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: 3 m (10 ft)
 Targets: Small or Medium corpse

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 11: Cleric, Warlock, and Wizard

Granted by items:

  • Crypt Lord Ring ( Recharge: Long rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • There are no scrolls available for wizards to transcribe to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • If the caster unprepares this spell, the summoned undead remains.
