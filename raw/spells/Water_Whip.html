Water Whip

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Water Whip is an action that allows a Way of the Four Elements monk to either knock a target prone, or pull it closer.

Description

Possibly pulls a target 9 m (30 ft) toward you or knocks it  Prone.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  2 Ki Points
Damage: 3~30
3d10⁠Bludgeoning
Details
 DEX Save (Weapon action DC) (On Save: Target still takes half damage but no other effects)
 Range: 9 m (30 ft)

At higher levels

At level 9 with  Improved Elemental Casting, the damage increases to 4d10⁠Bludgeoning.

Technical details

UIDs
Target_WaterWhip
The normal version accessible through Way of the Four Elements
Target_WaterWhip_Umberlee
Version used by Allandra Grey that does not used Ki points or grant a bonus unarmed strike
Spell flags
DisableBlood, IsHarmful, IsLinkedSpellContainer

Variants

  •  Water Whip: Knock Prone
  •  Water Whip: Pull

Grants:

  • Martial Arts: Bonus Unarmed Strike

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 3: Way of the Four Elements

Notes

  • Along with  Fangs of the Fire Snake (which uses an unarmed attack roll), this is the only other Way of Four Elements abilities that scales with Strength or Dexterity rather than Wisdom.
  • Since this uses weapon action DC, Heightened Manoeuvre from the  Gauntlets of the Warmaster will apply. Conversely, bonuses to spell save DC such as Lay Bare Their Weakness from the  Hat of Uninhibited Kushigo will not apply.

Bugs

  • The combat log will incorrectly show the DC calculation as the base DC + Wisdom modifier + proficiency bonus, but the resulting DC will still be based on the higher of the character's Dexterity or Strength modifier.
