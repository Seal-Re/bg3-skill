Aid

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Aid is a level 2 abjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to heal and embolden their allies and themselves with resolve. Affected creatures have their maximum Hit Points increased for the duration of the spell.

Description

Bolster your allies with toughness and resolve to heal and increase their Hit Points maximum.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 2 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self
 AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Radius

At higher levels

Upcasting: Casting this spell at a higher level increases the target's maximum health by an additional 5⁠⁠Hit Points for each spell slot level above 2nd.

Technical details

UID
Shout_Aid
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsSpell

Condition: Aid

 Aid

Duration: Until Long rest

  • Hit point maximum increased by 5⁠⁠Hit Points.
  • Hit point maximum increases by a further 5⁠⁠Hit Points for every Spell Slot Level above 2 used.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 3: Cleric and Life Domain (Domain Spell)
  • Class level 5: Paladin

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards cannot transcribe a  Scroll of Aid to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • Downed allies return back to the fight and heal an additional 1 hit point.
  • The  Aid condition remains even if the caster is removed from the party.
    • Given the persistence of both the Aid condition and the increased hit points it bestows (see below), it may be useful – particularly at higher levels – to have companions disguised and grouped together so an upcast version of this spell can be applied to all four intended active party members.
  • The increase to maximum hit points from this spell is distinct from  temporary hit points. Specifically, these hit points can be replenished with normal healing while temporary hit points cannot. Aid can stack with any single source of temporary hit points while sources of temporary hit points cannot stack with each other.
  • Alternate versions granted by items:
    •  Khalid's Gift: Aid from  Khalid's Gift and equivalent to Aid at 3rd level ( Recharge: Long rest)
    •  Shield of Devotion: Aid from  Shield of Devotion and equivalent to Aid at 3rd level ( Recharge: Long rest)
    •  Absolute's Talisman: Aid from  Absolute's Talisman and equivalent to Aid at 2nd level ( Recharge: Long rest)
  • When granted from an item, it only affects the wearer.
  • This is the only healing spell available to player characters that affects Undead and Constructs.
  • If cast by a Paladin or Cleric (non-Life Domain), this spell must remain Prepared; removing Aid from the caster's Prepared Spells removes the buff from all party members whom the caster applied Aid to, reverting their Maximum HP to its normal amount. If applied by using a scroll, the caster need not keep this spell Prepared to maintain the buff.
  • Multiple casts of Aid do not stack; the most recently cast instance takes precedence.
  • Despite the tool tip saying the spell heals the caster and their allies, the spell does not benefit from or trigger any items or class features that boost or interact with healing, such as  Disciple of Life or  Ring of Salving, unless the target is at 0 HP.
  • The incantations for Aid are Vita Excolatur ("Life is elevated") and Dum Vita Est Spes Est ("Where there is life, there is hope").
