Thunderwave

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
For the equivalent Way of the Four Elements Monk spell, see  Fist of Four Thunders.
Thunderwave is a level 1 evocation spell. It allows spellcasters to project out a deafening force that can hit multiple creatures and objects. Creatures hit by this force may be pushed away from the spellcaster.

Description

Release a wave of thunderous force that pushes away all creatures and objects in an area, while also dealing ⁠Thunder damage.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Damage: 2~16
2d8⁠Thunder ( Constitution Saving Throw to halve)
Details
 CON Save (On Save: Targets take half damage; aren't pushed away.)
 Melee: 1.5 m (5 ft)
 AoE: 5 m (17 ft) Cube
 Pushes: 8 m (27 ft)

At higher levels

Upcasting: Casting this spell at a higher level deals an extra 1d8⁠⁠Thunder damage for each spell slot level above 1st.

Technical details

UID
Zone_Thunderwave
Spell flags
AddFallDamageOnLand, HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsHarmful, IsSpell

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard, and Tempest Domain (Domain Spell)
  • Class level 3: Arcane Trickster and Eldritch Knight
  • Class level 6: Storm Sorcery (Storm Spell)
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Bard
  •  Magic Initiate: Druid
  •  Magic Initiate: Sorcerer
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard

Granted by items:

  • Ring of Absolute Force ( Recharge: Short rest)
  • Thunderpalm Strikers ( Recharge: Long rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards can transcribe a  Scroll of Thunderwave to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • If an affected creature passes the  Saving Throw, in addition to only taking half damage, they are also not pushed back.
  • This spell can be used to push opponents into chasms.
  • Thunderwave has the same incantation as  Shatter: Dētonō, Latin for "To expend one's thunder" or "To roar out".[1]
