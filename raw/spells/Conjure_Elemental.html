Conjure Elemental

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
This article is about the level 5 conjuration spell. For other uses, see Conjure Elemental (disambiguation).
Conjure Elemental is a level 5 conjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to summon a Fire, Earth, Water, or Air Elemental.

Description

Bend the barrier between the Planes until they disgorge an elemental ally to follow and fight for you.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 5 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)

At higher levels

At level 6, the spellcaster can summon an Air Myrmidon, Earth Myrmidon, Fire Myrmidon, or Water Myrmidon.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 9: Wizard, Druid, and Circle of the Land (Coast and Mountain)
  • Class level 10: Bard (via Magical Secrets)

Other ways to learn:

  • Warlocks who choose the Eldritch Invocation  Minions of Chaos at level 9+ learn this spell.
  • Wizards with  Level 5 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Conjure Elemental to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • Each individual caster can have only one active summon from this spell at a time. Casting it again while an Elemental or Myrmidon is already summoned replaces the existing one.
  • This spell is independent of  Conjure Minor Elemental. The same caster can have one instance of an Elemental or Myrmidon and a Minor Elemental active simultaneously.
  • The scroll version of the spell is cast at level 5 and as a result can only summon elementals and not myrmidons.
