Dethrone

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Dethrone is a level 5 necromancy spell. It is a unique spell which deals a devastating amount of ⁠Necrotic damage to a single target. Only wizards can learn this spell by transcribing it from a unique scroll.

Description

Shred a foe's very essence by pulling on strands of the Weave.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 5 Spell Slot
Damage: 30~80
10d6 + 20⁠Necrotic
Details
 CON Save (DC 18) (On Save: Targets still take half damage.)
 Range: 30 m (100 ft)
 Recharge: Long rest

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Projectile_CursedTome_Dethrone
Spell flags
AddFallDamageOnLand, HasHighGroundRangeExtension, HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsHarmful, IsSpell, RangeIgnoreVerticalThreshold

How to learn

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 5 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Dethrone to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • The save DC for this spell is always 18, regardless of the caster's actual spell save DC.
    • The DC remains 18 even when transcribed into and cast from a wizard's spellbook.
  • This spell can only be cast once per long rest, even when cast for free using a feature like  Arcane Battery or  Freecast.
  • With a range of 30 m (100 ft), this spell has the longest range in the game.
