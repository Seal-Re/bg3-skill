Devas are a type of Celestial being.

Involvement[edit section | visual editor]

Devas can be summoned to a spellcaster's aid with  Planar Ally or  Sights of the Seelie: Summon Deva from the  Scroll of Bestial Communion.

Notes[edit section | visual editor]

  • A hostile Deva spawns if a character affected by  Castigated By Divinity falls unconscious, is healed, rests, or has Remove Curse cast on them. Natively hostile Devas have  Extra Attack and 162 HP.
  •  Black Gauntlet Tamia Holzt can use  Planar Ally: Deva (Banite) to summon a unique Deva with red robes and black wings.
