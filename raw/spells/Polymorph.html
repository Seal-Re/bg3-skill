Polymorph

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Polymorph is a level 4 transmutation spell. It transforms a creature into a sheep.

Description

Transform a creature into a harmless sheep.

If the sheep's Hit Points drop to 0, the target reverts to its original form with its original Hit Points.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 7: Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard, Circle of the Land (Grassland), and Trickery Domain (Domain Spell)

Other ways to learn:

  • Also granted to Warlocks who choose the Eldritch Invocation  Sculptor of Flesh at level 7+.
  • Wizards with  Level 4 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Polymorph to their spellbooks.
