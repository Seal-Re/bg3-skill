Faerie Fire

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
See also: Drow Magic: Faerie Fire
Faerie Fire is a level 1 evocation spell. It allows spellcasters to conjure a brilliant light. Any creature caught in the radius temporarily sheds faint light and prevents  Invisibility.

Description

All targets within the light turn visible, and  Attack Rolls against them have  Advantage.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 DEX Save (Spell save DC)
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 AoE: 6 m (20 ft) Radius
 Concentration

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Bard, Druid, Light Domain (Domain Spell), and The Archfey
  • Class level 3: Swarmkeeper
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Bard
  •  Magic Initiate: Druid

Notes

  • Drow and Drow Half-Elf characters receive a unique variant at level 3,  Drow Magic: Faerie Fire, which they can cast once per Long Rest and does not cost a spell slot.

Related items[edit section | visual editor]

  •  Hellrider Longbow - Apply  Faerie Fire with a  DC 12 Dexterity Saving Throw when dealing damage from this weapon.
  •  Xyanyde - Once per short rest, apply  Faerie Fire on a miss with the weapon.
  •  Circlet of Hunting - Gain +1d4 bonus to attack rolls against creatures marked with  Faerie Fire.
