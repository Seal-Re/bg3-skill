Clench of the North Wind

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Clench of the North Wind is an action that allows a Way of the Four Elements monk to cast their equivalent of Hold Person by expending Ki Points instead of spell slots.

Description

Hold a humanoid enemy still. They can't move, act or react. Attacks from within 3 m (10 ft) are always critical hits.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  3 Ki Points
Details
 WIS Save
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 Concentration

At higher levels

At level 9, hold up to 2 humanoids.

Technical details

UID
Target_HoldPerson_Monk
Spell flags
HasHighGroundRangeExtension, HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsConcentration, IsHarmful

Condition: Hold Person

 Hold Person

Duration: 10 turns

 WIS Save (Spell save DC)

  • Affected entity can't Move or take Actions, Bonus Actions, or Reactions.
  • Attacks against the entity automatically succeed their Attack Roll.
  • Attacks against the entity are always Critical Hits if the attacker is within  Range: 3 m (10 ft).
  • Affected entity automatically fails all Strength and Dexterity  Saving Throws.
  • At the end of each turn, the affected entity makes a Wisdom  Saving Throw. On a successful save, the condition is removed.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 6: Way of the Four Elements

Notes

  • Unlike with an upcast  Hold Person, the targets of a level 9 Clench of the North Wind do not need to be distinct. This means a single target can be chosen twice, forcing it to make two initial saves. However, on subsequent turns, it will only need to succeed one saving throw to remove the  Hold Person effect since multiple instances of this effect do not stack.

Bugs

  • Unlike other Way of the Four Elements actions, this still retains its HasVerbalComponent SpellFlag from its parent spell Hold Person and thus cannot be used while  Silenced.
