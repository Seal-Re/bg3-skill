Mirror Image

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Mirror Image is a level 2 illusion spell. It allows spellcasters to create 3 illusory duplicates of themselves to distract attackers.

Description

Create 3 illusory duplicates of yourself to distract attackers. Each duplicate increases your  Armour Class by 3.

Whenever you successfully evade an attack, one of the duplicates disappears.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 2 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Shout_MirrorImage
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsSpell

Condition: Mirror Image

 Mirror Image

Duration: 10 turns

  • The mirror image distracts attackers, increasing the spellcaster's  Armour Class by 3 per duplicate image. When the caster successfully evades an attack, one of the images disappears.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 3: Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard, Trickery Domain (Domain Spell), and Circle of the Land (Coast and Mountain)
  • Class level 7: Arcane Trickster
  • Class level 8: Eldritch Knight

Granted by items:

  • Shelter of Athkatla

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 2 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Mirror Image to their spellbooks.
