Wall of Fire

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Wall of Fire is a level 4 evocation spell. It allows spellcasters to create a long, fiery barrier, damaging anything in the area.

Description

Create a blazing wall of fire,  Burning anyone who dares stand too close. Deals ⁠Fire to anything that moves into or ends its turn in the area.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 4 Spell Slot
Damage: 5~40
5d8⁠Fire
Details
 DEX Save (On Save: Targets still take half damage.)
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 Creates Area: Wall of Fire
 Concentration

At higher levels

Upcasting: Casting this spell at a higher level deals an extra 1d8⁠⁠Fire damage for each spell slot level above 4th.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 7: Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard, Light Domain (Domain Spell), The Fiend, and Circle of the Land (Desert)
  • Class level 10: Bard (via Magical Secrets)

Granted by items:

  • Markoheshkir ( Recharge: Short rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 4 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Wall of Fire to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This spell creates a wall from the first targeted point to the second targeted point, a distance of up to 36 m (120 ft). The wall is 3 m (10 ft) wide.
  • Does not break stealth.
  • The incantation for Wall of Fire is Ira Et Dolor, Latin for "Anger and pain".

Bugs

  • The spell save DC for damage received when ending a turn inside the wall is fixed at 10.
  • The upcast description does not reference fire damage, and merely states the spell deals "an additional 1d8 per level."
  • Concentration is not broken when the wall duration ends after 10 turns, creating a permanent source of Concentration for  Strange Conduit Ring.
  • Wall of Fire actually damages creatures within 2 m (7 ft).
