Feather Fall

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Feather Fall is a level 1 transmutation spell. It allows spellcasters to make themselves and their allies light as a feather, preventing damage when falling from extreme heights.

Description

You and nearby allies gain Immunity to Falling damage.

Properties

Cost
 Bonus Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self
 AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Radius
 Ritual

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Shout_FeatherFall
Spell flags
HasVerbalComponent, IsSpell, RangeIgnoreVerticalThreshold

Condition: Feather Fall

 Feather Fall

Duration: 10 turns

  • Rate of falling is slowed, granting Immunity to Falling damage.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard, and Draconic Bloodline (Silver/Cold Ancestry)
  • Class level 3: Arcane Trickster and Eldritch Knight
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Bard
  •  Magic Initiate: Sorcerer
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard

Granted by items:

  • Mystra's Grace
  • Corvid Token

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards can transcribe a  Scroll of Feather Fall to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This effect also prevents being knocked  Prone when falling from heights of greater than 8 m (27 ft).
