False Life

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
False Life is a level 1 necromancy spell. It allows spellcasters to gain Temporary Hit Points.

Description

Bolster yourself with a necromantic facsimile of life to gain  7 temporary hit points.

Can only have temporary hit points from one source.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self

At higher levels

Upcasting: Casting this spell at a higher level yields an extra  5 temporary hit points for each spell slot level above 1st.

Technical details

UID
Shout_FalseLife
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsSpell

Condition: False Life

 False Life

Duration: Until Long rest

  • Gain  7 temporary hit points plus 5 additional for each spell slot level above the first.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Sorcerer, Wizard, and Death Domain (Domain Spell)
  • Class level 3: Arcane Trickster and Eldritch Knight
  • Class level 6: College of Lore (via Magical Secrets)
  • Class level 10: Bard (via Magical Secrets)
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Sorcerer
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard

Other ways to learn:

  • Also granted to Warlocks who choose the Eldritch Invocation  Fiendish Vigour at level 2+. When gained in this way, the spell can be cast at will and doesn't expend Spell Slots.
  • Wizards can transcribe a  Scroll of False Life to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • If a wizard casts False Life on themselves, this spell must remain prepared. Removing it from the Prepared Spells list immediately removes the applied bonus. If applied by using a scroll directly, instead of transcribing it into a wizard's spellbook, the caster need not keep the spell prepared.
  • The incantation for False Life is Dum Vita Est, Spes Est, Latin for "While there is life, there is hope".
