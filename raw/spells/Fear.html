Fear

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Fear is a level 3 illusion spell. It allows spellcasters to terrify targets by projecting phantasmal images, forcing enemies to flee.

Description

Hostile targets drop everything and become  Fearful: they have  Disadvantage on Ability Checks and Attack Rolls.

If the target ends their turn where they can't see you, they can make another  Saving Throw to shake off their fear.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 3 Spell Slot
Details
 WIS Save
 Range: Self
 AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Cone
 Concentration

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

Condition: Fearful

 Fearful

Duration: 2 turns

 WIS Save (Spell save DC)

  • Has  Disadvantage on Ability Checks and  Attack Rolls.
  • It must run from the source of its fear and cannot take any additional actions.
  • Targets can make a  Saving Throw to shake off this effect if they end their turn out of sight from their source of fear.

Condition: Weapon Dropped!

 Weapon Dropped!

Duration: 1 turn

 WIS Save (Spell save DC)

  • An enemy has made you drop your weapon to the ground, similar to  Disarmed.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 5: Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard, and Trickery Domain (Domain Spell)
  • Class level 9: Gloom Stalker

Granted by items:

  • Servitor of the Black Hand Gloves ( Recharge: Long rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 3 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Fear to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This spell does not target allies who are included in the area of effect.
  • This spell is the only ability in the game that applies Fearful (or a variation of) but is affected by effects providing advantage or disadvantage on saving throws against being  Frightened, such as  Countercharm or  Shield of the Undevout.
    • While the initial saving throw to apply Fearful is affected by these effects, the saving throw that occurs each turn to remove Fearful is not.
