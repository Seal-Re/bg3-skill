Conjure Woodland Being

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Conjure Woodland Being is a level 4 conjuration spell. It summons a Dryad to fight alongside the caster.

Description

Conjure a dryad to fight alongside you. She can use  Nature's Step,  Entangle enemies, and  Summon a wood woad.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 4 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

 Strengthened Shillelagh ()

 Fallen Lover ()
Summon a Wood Woad bound to your tree to fight alongside you. ( Recharge: Short rest.)
 Spike Growth ()
Shape a piece of ground into hard spikes. A creature walking on the spikes takes 2d4⁠⁠Piercing damage for every 1.5 m (5 ft) it moves. The spikes are Difficult Terrain, halving a creature's Movement Speed.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 7: Druid

Notes

  • A  Wood Woad summoned by the Dryad remains intact and under player control if the Dryad is killed.
  • Each caster can only have one active instance of this spell, and each Dryad can only have one actively summoned Wood Woad; casting it again despawns the previous one.
