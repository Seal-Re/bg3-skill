Insect Plague

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
This article is about the spell. For other uses, see Insect Plague (disambiguation).
Insect Plague is a level 5 conjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to create a damaging, debilitating swarm of insects over a section of the battlefield.

Description

Locusts attack everyone within range, make the area Difficult Terrain, and impose  Disadvantage on Perception Checks.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 5 Spell Slot
Damage: 4~40
4d10⁠Piercing
Details
 CON Save (On Save: Targets still take half damage.)
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 AoE: 6 m (20 ft) Radius
 Creates Area: Insect Plague
 Concentration

At higher levels

Upcasting: Casting this spell at a higher level deals an extra 1d10⁠⁠Piercing damage for each spell slot level above 5th.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 9: Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Nature Domain (Domain Spell), Tempest Domain (Domain Spell), and Circle of the Land (Desert/Grassland/Swamp)

Bugs

  • Despite not dealing any ⁠Poison damage, this spell is tagged as a poison DamageType.
  • When first cast on an enemy, the combat log displays the enemy as making two separate saving throw rolls.
  • After the initial cast, subsequent turns of Insect Plague use the Constitution modifier of the caster, not the spellcasting modifier, in order to determine Difficulty Class.
