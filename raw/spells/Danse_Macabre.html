Danse Macabre

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Danse Macabre is a level 5 necromancy spell. It summons 4 uncontrollable Ghouls to fight for you.

Description

Create 4 ghouls that fight alongside you.

How to learn

Notes

  • This spell can only be acquired by completing the quest ⁠Unlock the Ancient Tome, in Act Three, which fully unlocks the secrets of the  Necromancy of Thay.
  • Unlike  Animate Dead: Ghoul Pack (or other Animate Dead spells), this spell does not require corpses to cast.
  • The Necromancy School features  Undead Thralls: Additional Undead and  Undead Thralls: Better Summons both apply to Danse Macabre. With these features, it can summon up to 5 ghouls each with more hit points and damage.
  •  Ghouls summoned by this spell:
    • Explode with a Death Burst when killed, dealing ⁠Necrotic area-of-effect damage. This can destroy other ghouls as a chain reaction and hurt nearby party members as well.
    • Are immune to  Demonspirit Madness. This prevents them from being driven mad due to a failed Wisdom saving throw when the summoner wears  Abyss Beckoners.
    • Cannot be controlled by the player, and can sometimes 'split' player-controlled characters into different sections of the Initiative order (e.g. Player character/Gale/Astarion/Ghoul/Shadowheart). This prevents the player from swapping between party members before the ghoul and party members after the ghoul (e.g. they cannot have Shadowheart cast  Create Water before Gale casts a spell with ⁠Lightning damage). This is a potentially large downside to using Danse Macabre.
    • Act simultaneously since they belong to the same swarm group, if in the same section of the initiative order. This can potentially reduce turn times due to the large number of extra creatures on the battlefield.
    • Continue to attack a  Knocked Out target for 0 damage and do not switch targets so long as the target remains knocked out.
  • The incantation for Danse Macabre is Surgē, the Latin command "Rise!"

Bugs

  • The tooltip incorrectly states it costs a  Level 5 Spell Slot.
