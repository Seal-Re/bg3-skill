Silence

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
For the equivalent Way of Shadow Monk action, see  Shadow Arts: Silence.
Silence is a level 2 illusion spell. It allows spellcasters to summon a sphere of absolute quiet in an area within range. Creatures within this sphere are immune to ⁠Thunder damage and cannot cast verbal spells.

Description

Create a sound-proof sphere. All within are  Silenced and Immune to ⁠Thunder damage.

Using this spell might turn targets hostile.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 3: Bard, Cleric, and Circle of the Land (Desert)
  • Class level 5: Ranger and Oath of Devotion (Oath Spell)

Other ways to learn:

  • Also granted to Warlocks who choose the Eldritch Invocation  Book of Ancient Secrets at level 7+. When gained in this way, the spell can be cast once per long rest and doesn't expend Spell Slots.

Notes

  • Silence does not affect  Counterspell.
  • Silence prevents initiation of dialogue with affected characters/entities.
  • Casting this spell in area that encompasses neutral NPCs may start a fight.
  • Silence has two incantations: Silencio, Latin for "Silence", and Te Astringo Lingua, Latin for "I bind your tongue".
