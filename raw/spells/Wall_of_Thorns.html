Wall of Thorns

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Wall of Thorns is a level 6 conjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to conjure a wall of brambles, Entangling and damaging any creatures who are in the area, or trying to pass through.

Description

Create a wall of pliable, twisting thorns surrounded by  Entangling vines.

Creatures can move through the wall, but take 7d8⁠⁠Piercing damage per turn, their Movement Speed is quartered.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 11: Druid

Used by creatures: Harvard Willoughby (Nature's Vengeance)

Notes

  • This spell creates a wall from the first targeted point to the second targeted point, a distance of up to 36 m (120 ft). The wall is 4 m (13 ft) wide.

Bugs

  • The in-game tooltip says the duration is 1 turn, but this is incorrect: it can last until Long Rest.
  • Spell save DC for Wall of Thorns is fixed at 3, and Entangling vines is at 12.
  • Movement speed is halved instead of quartered.
