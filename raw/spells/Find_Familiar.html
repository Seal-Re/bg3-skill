Find Familiar

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
See also: List of summoned familiars
Find Familiar is a level 1 conjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to conjure a fey familiar that can aid them in combat and world exploration.

Description

Summon a familiar, a fey spirit that takes an animal form of your choosing

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 Recharge: Short rest
 Ritual

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Wizard and Ranger (via Beast Tamer)
  • Class level 3: Eldritch Knight, Arcane Trickster, and Warlock (via Pact of the Chain)
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard
  •  Ritual Caster: Free Spells

Other ways to learn:

  • There are no scrolls available for wizards to transcribe to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • Although listed as a Ritual spell where one might expect otherwise, this spell - as per its description - has the limitation of being cast only once per short rest, per character. The standard (non-racial) version of the cantrip Mage Hand has the same limitation.
  •  Find Familiar: Cheeky Quasit and the version of Find Familiar granted from the  Ritual Caster feat have separate once per short rest limitations. A character who knows multiple versions of these spells can summon familiars multiple times (once for each version known) without short resting, though they are still limited to one active familiar at a time.
  • Familiars are automatically dismissed when the caster leaves the active party.
  • When cast by a Wizard, a conjured familiar persists if the caster unprepares the spell and unlike most summons, persists even after a long rest.
  • All versions and variants of the Find Familiar spell are mutually exclusive; a character can only have one familiar summoned by any Find Familiar spell at a time, including special summons like Shovel and Scratch, the  Raven Gloves spell  Summon Quothe the Raven, and the additional options granted to a Warlock with  Pact of the Chain. However, this does not include Us, as it does not count as a Find Familiar variant. Casting another version of the spell dismisses any currently summoned familiar.
  • The Beast Master Ranger feature  Summon Companion is separate to Find Familiar, and may also be active at the same time.
  • Familiars can drink elixirs and potions by having someone else place elixirs/potions out of their inventory and then have the familiar drink it from the ground by right-clicking them, or by drinking it directly from a companion's inventory as long as the elixir/potion is not inside a container.
  • Even though familiars can drink elixirs, increasing their Strength serves no purpose outside of increasing jump distance, as their attacks always use Dexterity.
  • The raven is the only standard familiar given a specific name: "Quothe". This is a reference to Edgar Allen Poe's poem "The Raven", and probably also the raven Quoth who appears in Terry Pratchett's Discworld novels (himself a reference to "The Raven").
  • Familiars cannot interact with the environment except to examine and attack. Notably, unlike Mage Hand, they cannot be used to explore burrows or press buttons (though many of the buttons and levers can still be triggered if the Familiar Attacks them). Familiars can drag objects. This includes placing objects onto vents or moving objects off of traps.
  • The incantation for standard Find Familiar variants is Ex Textura, Latin for "Out of the weave", while that of  Find Familiar: Boo is Invoco Te, Latin for "I call you".
