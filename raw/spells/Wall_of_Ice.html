Wall of Ice

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Wall of Ice is a level 6 evocation spell. It creates a wall of ice that deals strong Cold damage to anyone nearby.

Description

Raise a wall of solid ice that deals 10d6⁠⁠Cold damage to anyone standing in its way.

When the ice is broken, it leaves behind a cloud of frigid air that deals 10d6⁠⁠Cold per turn to creatures within.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 6 Spell Slot
Damage: 20~120
10d6⁠Cold
+ 10d6⁠Cold (When the wall breaks)
Details
 DEX Save (On Save: Targets still take half damage.)
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 Creates Area: Wall of Ice
 Concentration

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 11: Wizard

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 6 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Wall of Ice to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • Spell save DC for initial damage is fixed at 15 (Dexterity) while the spell save DC for Ice Cloud damage is fixed at 16 (Constitution). Spell save DC is not modified by bonus DC from caster's equipment or elixir.
