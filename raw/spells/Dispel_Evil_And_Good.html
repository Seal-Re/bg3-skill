Dispel Evil And Good

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Dispel Evil And Good is a level 5 abjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to protect themselves from Evil and Good creatures, and allows them to break Enchantments on allies.

Description

Aberrations[See: Bugs], Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, and Undead have  Disadvantage on  Attack Rolls against you.

You can also break Enchantments that  Charm,  Frighten, or  Possesses allies.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 5 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self
 Concentration

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

  • Additionally, it can also break otherworldly Enchantments that  Charm,  Frighten, or Possesses allies.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 9: Cleric

Bugs

  • Despite the description, this spell lacks any function versus Aberrations.
