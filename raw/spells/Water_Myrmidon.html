Water Myrmidons are a type of Elemental creature that can be transformed into via  Combat Wild Shape, or summoned with the spell  Conjure Elemental.

About Water Myrmidons[edit section | visual editor]

Water Myrmidons are created by conjuring and bonding elemental water into ritually created armour.

Notes[edit section | visual editor]

  • If thrown into a water chasm, Water Myrmidons float back into safety.
  • Water Myrmidon creatures have a base initiative of 2 instead of the usual 0. With a Dexterity modifier of 2, this gives +4 initiative total. This extra initiative does not apply to wild shape form.
