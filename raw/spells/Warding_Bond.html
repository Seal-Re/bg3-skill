Warding Bond

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Warding Bond is a level 2 abjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to bolster an ally's defenses while also sharing any damage they receive.

Description

Ward an ally. They gain resistance to all damage, and a +1 bonus to their  Armour Class and  Saving Throws.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 2 Spell Slot
Details
 Melee: 1.5 m (5 ft)

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Target_WardingBond
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsMelee, IsSpell

Condition: Warding Bond (Target)

 Warding Bond

Duration: Until Long rest

  • Affected entity has a +1 bonus to  Armour Class and  Saving Throws, and Resistance to all damage.
  • Each time it takes damage, the spellcaster takes the same amount of damage.

Condition: Warding Bond (Caster)

 Warding Bond

Duration: Until Long rest

  • Creature is bound to an ally. Each time the ally takes damage, the creature takes the same amount of damage.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 3: Cleric
  • Class level 5: Oath of the Crown (Oath Spell)

Granted by items:

  • Viconia's Walking Fortress ( Recharge: Long rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • Also granted by the ring  True Love's Embrace. The spell can only be cast once per Long Rest, and the target must be wearing the matching ring,  True Love's Caress.

Notes

  • This spell can be active on multiple allies at once. In such cases, the caster redirects damage from all allies who have received Warding Bond from them.
  • Damage redirected by Warding Bond triggers a  Concentration  Saving Throw if the caster is concentrating on another spell.
  • Damage redirected by Warding Bond can be reduced by the caster's resistances (if any).
  • Warding Bond remains active on the target party member if the caster is  Downed, but is removed if the caster dies.
  • Warding Bond has no effect on a party member who has already cast Warding Bond.
    • If multiple party members cast Warding Bond on each other, Warding Bond will not activate for either party member (Resistances and bonuses to Armour Class and Saving Throws are removed, and damage is not redirected).
    • If a party member casts Warding Bond on another party member, and that party member then casts Warding Bond on a third party member, Warding Bond only remains active for the third party member (who has not cast Warding Bond).
  • Warding Bond persists if the caster is dismissed from the party back to camp. In such a case, the caster continues to receive damage redirected from their bonded target, and will be damaged (or potentially dead) while at camp.
  • This spell must remain Prepared by the caster; removing this spell from the caster's Prepared Spells list removes the buff from themselves and the party members they cast Warding Bond on.
    • This feature can be used to remove existing Warding Bond cast by using above mentioned items without long resting.

Bugs

  • If the target party member is in  Wild Shape and has the  Tavern Brawler feat, damage shared to the caster will be increased according to the Tavern Brawler bonus, resulting in high extra damage taken for the caster.
  • Warding Bond places a passive on the caster which, among other text, states that the spell ends if "...you and the target become separated by more than ." Despite the lack of a stated distance, no such mechanic exists.
