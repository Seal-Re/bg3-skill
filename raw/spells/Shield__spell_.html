Shield (spell)

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
For the mundane item, see  Shield. For the equipment type, see Shields (equipment).
Shield is a level 1 abjuration spell. It allows spellcasters to avoid being hit by an enemy attack at the last possible instant. It also nullifies Magic Missile damage against them.

Description

When you are about to be hit by an enemy, use your Reaction to increase your  Armour Class by 5. You also take no damage from  Magic Missile. These effects last until the start of your next turn.

Properties

Cost
 Reaction +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Sorcerer, Wizard, and The Hexblade
  • Class level 3: Arcane Trickster and Eldritch Knight
Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Sorcerer
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard

Granted by items:

  • Amulet of the Harpers ( Recharge: Long rest)
  • Shield of Shielding ( Recharge: Long rest)
  • Elegant Studded Leather ( Recharge: Short rest)

Other ways to learn:

  • There are no scrolls available for wizards to transcribe to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This spell does not trigger  Spell Rot.
  • If a character with more than one class using  Charisma as its Spellcasting ability wants this spell (e.g.,  Sorcerer and  The Hexblade) both classes can select it.
  • While the  Wizard,  Arcane Trickster and  Eldritch Knight classes share the same Spellcasting ability ( Intelligence), this spell cannot be picked multiple times via these classes on the same character, following these rules:
    • A  Wizard selection will always be replaced by the two others as it is considered less efficient.
    • Once selected by either  Arcane Trickster or  Eldritch Knight, it will not be available for the other.

Bugs

  • Due to a coding error, Shield does not grant charges of  Arcane Ward.
  • Due to an incomplete list of spell checks, Shield will not reduce the damage of Magic Missiles from  Illithid Arcanists for the rest of the turn after Shield is used as a reaction.
  • When provided by an item, Shield does not provide any beneficial effects which would otherwise normally last until the start of the caster's next turn.
