Speak with Animals

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
This article is about the spell. For the condition, see Speak with Animals (condition). For Wild Magic feature, see Wild Magic: Speak with Animals. For other uses, see Speak with Animals (disambiguation).
Speak with Animals is a level 1 divination spell. It allows spellcasters to understand and talk to beasts until they long rest.

Description

Gain the ability to comprehend and communicate with beasts.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 1 Spell Slot
Details
 Range: Self
 Ritual

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Shout_SpeakWithAnimals
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsSpell

Condition: Speak with Animals

 Speak with Animals

Duration: Until Long rest

  • Can comprehend and communicate with beasts.

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Bard, Druid, and Nature Domain (Domain Spell)
  • Class level 2: Ranger
  • Class level 3: Oath of the Ancients (Oath Spell) and Wildheart
  • Class level 6: Knowledge Domain
Races:
  • Character level 1: Forest Gnome

(Character level is the sum of all class levels for a multi-classed character.)

Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Bard
  •  Magic Initiate: Druid
  •  Ritual Caster: Free Spells

Granted by items:

  • Potion of Animal Speaking

Other ways to learn:

  • Also granted to Warlocks who choose the Eldritch Invocation  Beast Speech at level 2+. This version of the spell does not expend Spell Slots.

Notes

  • Wildheart Barbarians can cast this spell once per Long Rest.
  • Forest Gnomes can cast this spell once per Long Rest.
  • Knowledge Domain clerics cast this spell using a  Channel Divinity Charge instead of a spell slot.
  • Druids must keep this spell prepared, or the effect will be lost.
  • The incantations for Speak with Animals are Amicus Animales ("Friend of animals") and Loque Sentibi Plus ("Speak! I will feel more.") 'Sentibi' does not represent a Latin grammatical form.
  • Speak with Animals does not work on shapechangers when they are in animal form.
