Mud Mephits are an Elemental creature and type of mephit.

Involvement[edit section | visual editor]

Curious and impish creatures from the Elemental Planes, they are quite mischievously territorial and often work with other elemental or natural beings. Mud Mephits can be created with the spell  Conjure Minor Elemental.
