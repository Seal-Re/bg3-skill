Circle of Death

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Circle of Death is a level 6 necromancy spell. It devastates all creatures within a large radius with ⁠Necrotic energy.

Description

Sculpt a massive sphere of entropic energy around a creature. Devastate the target and all surrounding creatures.

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 6 Spell Slot
Damage: 8~48
8d6⁠Necrotic
Details
 CON Save (On Save: Targets still take half damage.)
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Radius
 Targets: All creatures in AoE centred around target creature

At higher levels

Casting this spell at a higher level grants no additional benefit.

Technical details

UID
Target_CircleOfDeath
Spell flags
HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsHarmful, IsSpell

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 11: Sorcerer, Warlock, and Wizard

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 6 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Circle of Death to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • Unlike other area spells such as  Fireball which can target the ground, Circle of Death must specifically target a creature. This can make positioning the area of effect to avoid friendly fire much more difficult.
