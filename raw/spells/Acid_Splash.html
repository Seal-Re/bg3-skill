Acid Splash

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Acid Splash is a cantrip (Conjuration). It deals ⁠Acid damage to enemies in a small area.

Description

Throw a bubble of acid that damages each creature it hits.

Properties

Cost
 Action
Damage: 1~6
1d6⁠Acid ( Dexterity Saving Throw to negate)
Details
 DEX Save
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 AoE: 2 m (7 ft) Radius

At higher levels

  • At character level 5, the damage increases to 2d6⁠⁠Acid.
  • At character level 10, the damage increases to 3d6⁠⁠Acid.

Technical details

UID
Projectile_AcidSplash
Spell flags
CanAreaDamageEvade, HasHighGroundRangeExtension, HasSomaticComponent, HasVerbalComponent, IsHarmful, IsSpell, RangeIgnoreVerticalThreshold

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 1: Sorcerer and Wizard
  • Class level 3: Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster
Races:
  • Character level 1: High Elf and High Half-Elf

(Character level is the sum of all class levels for a multi-classed character.)

Granted by features:
  •  Magic Initiate: Sorcerer
  •  Magic Initiate: Wizard

Other ways to learn:

  • There are no scrolls available for wizards to transcribe to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • The incantation for Acid Splash is Acido, Latin for "acid."
