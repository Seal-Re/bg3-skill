Phantasmal Killer

From bg3.wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
Phantasmal Killer is a level 4 illusion spell. It allows spellcasters to temporarily haunt a creatures mind with illusions, dealing damage, preventing movement, and imposing Disadvantage on Ability Checks and Attack Rolls.

Description

Haunt a creature with illusions of its greatest fears. It takes 4d10⁠⁠Psychic Damage per turn, cannot move, has  Disadvantage on Ability Checks and Attack Rolls

Properties

Cost
 Action +  Level 4 Spell Slot
Damage: 4~40
4d10⁠Psychic (Per turn)
Details
 WIS Save
 Range: 18 m (60 ft)
 Concentration

At higher levels

Upcast: When this spell is cast at 5th level or higher, it deals an additional 1d10⁠⁠Psychic damage per level per turn.

 Phantasmal Killer

 WIS Save (Spell save DC)

How to learn

Classes:
  • Class level 7: Wizard and The Hexblade

Other ways to learn:

  • Wizards with  Level 4 Spell Slots can transcribe a  Scroll of Phantasmal Killer to their spellbooks.

Notes

  • This ability is not affected by effects providing advantage or disadvantage on saving throws against being  Frightened, such as  Countercharm or  Shield of the Undevout.
